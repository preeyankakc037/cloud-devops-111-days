## Key Connections

Deployments and Pods
Deployments manage pods and ensure the application is always running.

Services and Networking
Services provide stable access and enable communication between components.

ConfigMaps and Secrets
They manage configuration and sensitive data separately from the application.

Namespaces
They help organize and separate different environments.

Persistent Storage
Ensures data is not lost even if pods restart.

Ingress
Acts as a single entry point for external traffic.
